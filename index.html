The strategy, in plain English

Task One

Three things. That is the whole strategy.

What you do not need: another fifteen treatments, thousands a month into Facebook, or to market like a big-city clinic. You are in a town of about 15,000 people. That changes everything, and it is an advantage a Brisbane clinic cannot copy.

1. Own Moranbah

Marketing in a town this size cannot live only online. The Aesthetic Advisory needs to be everywhere people already are: the sporting clubs, the school fundraisers, the gym, the hairdresser, the boutique, the women's networking group, the mining company's community days. Long-term partnerships, not 500 discount vouchers. Google owned now, before someone else does it. Reviews that mention the Skin Reset and the Observ, because that is what people already talk about at the pub and at school pick-up.

2. Fill the $249.49 Skin Reset

Stop marketing machines. Nobody in Moranbah sits at home wondering whether she needs IPL, Evo or DermaFrac. She knows her pigmentation is annoying her, her skin is dry, her pores look huge, her acne keeps coming back, or her skin looks terrible after seven days at the mine. Every ad, page and post starts there and points at one thing: the Skin Reset. And we film the Skin Reset itself, start to finish, because the experience is the ad. Show her why it is worth $249.

3. Systemise it so it can be replicated

The Skin Reset already works. The back end takes too long and the follow-up lives in people's heads. We write down exactly what happens, step by step, turn it into scripts, templates and automations, put a CRM around Timely, and measure the numbers that matter, Skin Club above all. Then everything we build passes one test: can another team, another therapist, another coordinator in another town run it without you in the room? Yes means a business model. No means a busy clinic.

The bottleneck

The report in minutes, presented while she is still excited.

The client experience is working. The back end is not: 30 to 45 minutes after she has gone. You want it under 30, produced while she is under the LED. We think it is under ten. We do not touch the personalised part, because that is why the Skin Reset converts. We remove the rewriting of the same pigmentation explanation fifteen times a week.

She sits back down and sees her skin, her plan and her homecare, then it is sent to her phone, the Skin Club is offered and the next appointment is booked before she leaves.
